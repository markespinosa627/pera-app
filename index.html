About BADYET
Why this exists

Created by Mark Espinosa, a multidisciplinary artist and creator. (markespinosa.com)

I designed BADYET (Budget Allocation for Daily Yields & Expense Tracking) specifically with the everyday Filipino worker in mind. Many of us live paycheck to paycheck, relying heavily on the traditional 15th and 30th (kinsenas-katapusan) payroll cycle to juggle bills, debts, and daily expenses.

Traditional budgeting apps are often built on monthly cycles that simply don't fit our reality. BADYET bridges this gap, allowing you to align your budget perfectly with when you actually get paid, so you can achieve peace of mind, manage your debts effectively, and build a clearer financial future without the mental gymnastics.
